# Business Drivers (Ranked)

| Rank | Driver | Description | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reduce fraud losses and chargebacks | Lower fraud and chargeback ratio while maintaining approval rate | Head of Payments |
| 2 | Avoid sanctions and customs penalties | Pre-fulfilment screening; correct HS classification | Trade Compliance |
| 3 | Enable market entry in new jurisdictions | Regional cells; residency-aware design | Growth / Product |
| 4 | Protect brand trust | Fair, explainable, consistent decisions | Product / Trust |
| 5 | Keep checkout conversion high | Fraud scoring within p99 < 150 ms budget | Growth |
| 6 | Reduce compliance scope and audit cost | Tokenize; regionalize data | CISO / DPO |

## Notes

- Ranked drivers set the priority order used in requirements and the decision matrix.
- Any conflict (e.g., conversion vs fraud friction) is resolved in favour of the higher-ranked driver unless a regulator forces otherwise.
