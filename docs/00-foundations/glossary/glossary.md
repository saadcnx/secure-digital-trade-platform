# Glossary

## Trade

HS code, WCO Data Model, SAFE Framework, Incoterms, AEO, denied party list, SDN list, end-use/end-user controls, certificate of origin.

## Payments

PAN, tokenization vs encryption, CDE, SAQ, 3-D Secure 2, SCA/PSD2, chargeback, card testing, acquirer/issuer, FX.

## Fraud / ML

Velocity features, device fingerprint, precision/recall, false-positive cost, concept drift, model card, feature store.

## Privacy

Lawful basis, Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interests, DPIA (Art. 35), Art. 22 automated decisions, SCCs, TIA, data residency, pseudonymisation vs anonymisation, CPRA.

## Architecture

C4, ADR, DFD, trust boundary, saga, outbox, idempotency, circuit breaker, RPO/RTO, SLO.

## Accuracy notes

- **PCI DSS v4.0.1** is the current version. v4.0 was retired; future-dated requirements became mandatory 31 March 2025.
- Always cite article/requirement numbers when referencing a regulation.
